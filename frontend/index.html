AI System Architecture

FreshTrack AI employs a Multi-Task Transfer Learning Network built on a MobileNetV2 backbone pretrained on ImageNet. It features two parallel classification heads:

1. Produce Classification Head

Predicts probabilities across 14 fruit and vegetable categories (Apple, Banana, Orange, Tomato, Potato, Carrot, Bell Pepper, Broccoli, Strawberry, Cucumber, Onion, Lettuce, Avocado, Lemon).

2. Freshness State Head

Estimates visual surface condition into 3 distinct categories: Fresh, Fair / Consume Soon, and Overripe / Spoiled.

How to Train Your Own Model in Google Colab

  1. Open the training notebook located at:
    notebooks/FreshTrack_AI_Training_TransferLearning.ipynb in Google Colab.
  2. Set the runtime hardware accelerator to T4 GPU (Runtime > Change runtime type > T4 GPU).
  3. Execute all cells to fine-tune the MobileNetV2 transfer learning weights.
  4. Download the exported weights file: freshtrack_model.pth.
  5. Place freshtrack_model.pth in your backend directory:
    FreshTrackAI/backend/models/freshtrack_model.pth.
  6. Restart your FastAPI server. The app automatically recognizes the file and transitions from Demo Mode to Active PyTorch Model!

Ethical AI & Food Safety Compliance

In accordance with food safety standards, FreshTrack AI communicates the operational boundary of computer vision systems: optical imaging can only estimate external surface characteristics (pigment shifts, spotting, wrinkling). It does not guarantee bacterial sterility or food safety. The interface prominently highlights calibrated uncertainty scores and safety disclaimers on every scan.
